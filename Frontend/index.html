🏥 About Us

Parul Sevashram Hospital

Founded in 2012 under Parul Arogya Seva Mandal Trust, PSH is a NABH-accredited digital hospital in Vadodara committed to "Health for All." Spread across 4.2 lakh sq ft, we serve patients from across Gujarat, MP and Rajasthan.

1100+ beds across multispeciality & superspeciality departments
NABH & NABL accredited — awarded Leading Hospital, West Zone at Delhi National Healthcare Excellence Awards
PMJAY-MAA empanelled — cashless treatment for eligible patients, coverage up to ₹5 lakh/year
24-hour pharmacy, lab & diagnostics — X-ray, sonography, echo, and more on-site
